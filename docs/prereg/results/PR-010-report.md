## The hypothesis, and what happened to it

Registered: *EDGE's estimate on the eligible universe exceeds its zero-spread floor at this
universe's measured volatility, by enough to set `costs.slippage_model`.*

**Refuted.** §6 fixed `reject` at "median at or below the floor". The floor was **41.87bp per side**,
calibrated before the run. The median is **25.65bp**.

## Result

1,134 eligible instruments, none refused, EDGE's underlying estimate negative on 214 of them.

| | bp per side |
|---|---|
| p25 | 16.81 |
| **median** | **25.65** |
| p75 | 39.17 |
| p95 | 87.00 |
| **calibrated zero-spread floor** | **41.87** |

**Three quarters of the universe reads below the floor.**

## The agreement that is not corroboration

`DR-005` measured **25.44bp** with Abdi-Ranaldo. EDGE reports **25.65bp**. Two estimators of
different construction, one built specifically to correct the other's bias, landing 0.21bp apart.

That looks like corroboration and is not, for two reasons fixed before the run:

1. **Both sit under their own zero-spread floors.** At measured volatility — overnight sd 2.992%,
   intraday 3.579% — Abdi-Ranaldo reads **33.85bp** on a spreadless series and EDGE reads
   **41.87bp**. Each estimator reports *less* on the real universe than it reports on nothing.
   Two instruments agreeing inside their common noise is what shared bias looks like, not what
   independent measurement looks like.
2. **Neither declines with liquidity.** By ADTV third, EDGE reads 25.45 / 27.90 / 24.02 — flat, and
   the most liquid third is the *lowest* by 1.4bp against a spread that should differ by an order of
   magnitude across that range. `PR-008` found the same flatness in Abdi-Ranaldo (−0.02 against log
   ADTV), and the literature documents it as these estimators' known failure mode.

## What this establishes

**The spread level is not obtainable from daily OHLC on this universe.** Three estimators now, across
2012, 2017 and 2024, including the one built to fix the other two and the only one that reads the
open. `AGENTS.md` §10.3 sent us to the literature and the literature sent us to EDGE; EDGE fails the
same way, and now that is measured rather than assumed.

`PR-006` — real fills in a forward test — is the only remaining route to a measured cost. That was
`DR-004`'s claim when it reserved the id on 2026-08-02. It is now a finding.

## What this does not establish

**It does not say there is no spread.** `PR-008`'s sign test settles that there is, and it was
re-run for this study at *measured* overnight volatility after the first version used a synthetic
with no overnight gap at all: real bars clamp at **19.1%** against **51.5%** for spreadless
synthetic. The direction is settled and stays settled. Only the level is unobtainable.

**It does not overturn `DR-005`.** 25bp remains the best available estimate and remains `assumed`.
What changes is the honest reading: it is a number two estimators produce inside their shared noise
floor, not a measurement of 25. `DR-005`'s *Consequence for PR-005* is unaffected — the base
strategy's sign is robust across the whole plausible range, because break-even needs slippage under
**6.85bp per side** and nothing here reads anywhere near that low.

## Limitations

- **The floor is calibrated on synthetic bars**, matched to volatility measured from the store but
  generated by a model of our own. It is the best control available and it is not the market.
- **The fixture failed first, silently.** The open was initially faked as the previous close, which
  makes `o − c1` identically zero and returns a spread of zero for every input. It looked like a
  broken estimator. Recorded because the failure mode is invisible: a fixture that is wrong in a
  field only one estimator reads.
- **Survivorship absent**, and conservative here as in `PR-008`.
